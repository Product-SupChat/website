Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 · 12 sections · SupChat for iOS

This Privacy Policy explains how Zhuoqian Wang (“we”, “us”), an individual developer, handles information when you use SupChat (the “App”), an independent multi-account workspace that loads the official web clients of third-party messaging platforms, such as WhatsApp and Telegram, in isolated containers on your device.

The short version

The App has no backend servers, no account system, and no analytics. We do not collect your personal data, we cannot read your chats, and we have nowhere to send them.

01

Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data. Specifically:

  • No servers, no account system: the App operates entirely on your device.
  • No analytics and no advertising: the App contains no analytics SDKs and no ads, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites (for App Tracking Transparency purposes, we do not track you at all).
  • No payment data: subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your credit card number or bank information.
  • Support communications: if you contact us by email, we receive your email address and anything you send us, and use it only to respond to you.
02

Where Your Data Lives

Everything the App stores stays on your device, inside the App’s own sandbox:

  • Each account you add runs in its own isolated storage container on this device, separate from every other account.
  • Session state, cached conversations and media, and files you download remain inside the App’s sandbox and are removed when you delete the account or uninstall the App.
  • Your App Lock PIN is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.
03

What Leaves Your Device

The App loads the official web clients of messaging platforms (for example, WhatsApp Web and Telegram Web) directly from those platforms. When a web client runs, it communicates with that platform’s own servers — exactly as it would in a web browser on your device. That traffic goes directly from your device to the platform. It is never routed through us, and we have no access to it.

When you use features that read information from a platform (for example, syncing your profile nickname or avatar), the App reads that content on your device, processes it locally, and stores it only in the App’s sandbox.

04

Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store: processes subscription payments and refunds. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to any payment data Apple collects.
  • Messaging platforms: WhatsApp, Telegram, and any other platform whose web client you use inside the App are independent third-party services. Their own privacy policies and terms govern how they handle your information when you use them. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of them, and we have no access to their systems.

We do not use Firebase, Unity Analytics, AdMob, AppLovin, or any other analytics, advertising, or tracking service.

05

Permissions the App Requests

Each permission is used only for the feature named, and the related data stays on your device:

Camera and Microphone
For calls you place inside a platform’s web client. The media stream goes directly from your device to that platform, not through us.
Photos
To save media files you choose to download.
Notifications
Only for local reminders you create with the App’s reminder tool. The App does not send push notifications of any kind.
Face ID / Touch ID
To unlock the App’s privacy lock.
06

Your Controls

From the App’s settings you can, at any time:

  • Clear a single account’s cache or session data;
  • Clear data for all accounts at once;
  • Delete an account entirely, together with its isolated container; or
  • Uninstall the App, which removes all locally stored data.

You can also enable App Lock so the workspace cannot be opened without your PIN or biometrics, change the App’s home screen icon, and hide conversation details (avatars, names, message previews) in the account switcher.

07

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or a higher age where required by local law). Because we collect no personal data at all, we hold none from any age group. Please also note that the third-party messaging platforms you access through the App have their own age requirements, which apply to your use of those services.

08

Data Retention and Security

All data created or cached by the App is retained only on your device, until you delete it or uninstall the App. We take reasonable technical measures appropriate to an on-device application. Because we operate no servers and collect nothing, there is no central store of user data that could be breached.

09

Your Rights

Depending on your region (for example, the EU/EEA under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete, or correct your personal data. Because we do not collect personal data, there is ordinarily nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete on your behalf. Data on your device is under your control (see Section 6). If you believe we hold personal information about you (for example, because you emailed us), you may request access to or deletion of it using the contact address below.

10

Refunds

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple under the App Store’s refund policy. We have no servers, receive no purchase data, and play no part in refund decisions.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the “Effective Date” at the top of this page.
